# Privacy Policy for TimeMeasurer

**Effective date:** August 22, 2026

## 1. Introduction

TimeMeasurer ("we", "our", or "us") is a multi-timer and multi-stopwatch application that allows you to run an unlimited number of interval timers and stopwatches in parallel. This Privacy Policy describes what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

By using TimeMeasurer, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

TimeMeasurer does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the app. We do not collect, and the app does not require, the following:

- Name, email address, phone number, or other contact details
- Precise or approximate location data
- Contacts, call logs, messages, or media files
- Photos, videos, or audio files
- Financial information, health information, or payment details

The app is fully functional offline and does not require an internet connection for its core features.

## 3. Information We Collect

We collect only the limited, anonymous information described below. We do not collect any information that directly identifies you personally.

### 3.1 App activity

We collect anonymous information about how you use the app, such as which features are used (for example, starting, pausing, resetting, or stopping a timer or stopwatch). This data is used to understand usage patterns and improve the app.

### 3.2 App information and performance

We collect crash logs and diagnostics when the app encounters an error. This technical data (for example, device model, operating system version, and stack traces) is used solely to identify and fix technical issues.

### 3.3 Device or other identifiers

We may collect device identifiers for analytics and attribution purposes, including:

- Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android)
- Anonymous installation/app-instance identifiers used by our analytics providers

These identifiers are used for anonymous analytics, install attribution, and crash reporting. They are not linked to your name or other personal details.

## 4. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All of your app content — timers, stopwatches, laps, statistics, themes, and settings — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not synced to any server operated by us. You can delete this data at any time by deleting individual timers or stopwatches within the app, or by uninstalling the app.

## 5. Analytics

To improve the app, we use anonymous analytics services:

- Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC)
- Huawei Analytics Kit (Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd.)

These services collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics, such as which features are used (for example, starting, pausing, or resetting a timer or stopwatch) and technical information about your device (such as device model and operating system version). They may also collect device identifiers as described in section 3.3. This data is used solely to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It does not include your name, email address, or any other information that directly identifies you personally.

## 6. Crash Reporting

To help us identify and fix errors, the app uses crash reporting services:

- Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC)
- Huawei AGC Crash Service (Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd.)

When the app encounters an error, these services may collect technical diagnostic data, including device information and crash logs. This information is used exclusively to diagnose and resolve technical issues.

## 7. Permissions Used by the App

The app requests the following permissions solely to provide its functionality. No permission is used to collect personal information.

- **Notifications:** to show the status of running timers and stopwatches.
- **Foreground service:** to keep running timers and stopwatches active in the background.
- **Vibration:** to vibrate when a session or break starts or ends.
- **Keep screen on (Wake lock):** to keep the screen on while the app is open, if you enable this option.
- **Battery optimization:** to request permission to disable battery optimization so timers and stopwatches can run reliably in the background, if you enable this option.
- **Network access:** used only for analytics and crash reporting.
- **Advertising identifier and install referrer:** used for anonymous analytics and install attribution.

You can manage or revoke most of these permissions at any time through your device's system settings.

## 8. Notifications and Background Operation

TimeMeasurer uses notifications to keep you informed about running timers and stopwatches. To support this, the app may run a foreground service while a timer or stopwatch is active. The app does not use notifications for advertising or marketing.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party SDKs, which process data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

- Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) — https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Huawei Mobile Services (Analytics and Crash) — https://consumer.huawei.com/privacy/

## 10. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share any data with third parties except for the anonymous analytics and crash reporting described above, which are provided by Google and Huawei under their respective privacy terms. The app does not contain third-party advertising.

## 11. Data Retention and Deletion

Analytics and crash data are retained and managed by Google and Huawei according to their respective data retention policies. Because your timer, stopwatch, and settings data is stored only on your device, we have no access to it and cannot retain it. You can delete all locally stored data at any time by deleting individual timers or stopwatches within the app, or by uninstalling the app. For deletion of data held by our analytics or crash reporting providers, you may contact us using the details below.

## 12. Children's Privacy

TimeMeasurer is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

## 13. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Because your data is stored locally on your device, its security also depends on the security of your device and your device settings.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Effective date" at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
